## 🎨 Visual Identity & Inspiration

Crafted specifically based on editorial reference guidelines:
- **Reference 1 Palette & Typography**: Rich dark forest green (`#14231b`, `#0c1811`) and warm cream (`#f6f3eb`) dual-surface aesthetic, mustard/amber accents (`#e59f24`), terracotta accents (`#d9683e`), oversized serif display typography (`Playfair Display`), monospace code touches (`JetBrains Mono`), ghost watermarks, sparkles (`✦`), and Japanese Kanji motifs (`進歩`, `日本語`).
- **Reference 2 Modular Architecture**: Floating card compositions, high-contrast metric callouts, pill tags, consistent borders, and asymmetrical editorial layouts.
- **Strictly Non-Photographic**: Pure typography, abstract geometry, satellite floating badges, and high-contrast editorial layouts without portraits or human silhouettes.
- **Zero Slide Numbers & Zero Emojis**: Clean SVG vector iconography and typographic bullets throughout.
