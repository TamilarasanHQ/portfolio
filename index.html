BRIDGE: First-Attempt Delivery Risk Intelligence & Optimization Engine

ROLE: MACHINE LEARNING ENGINEER & FULL-STACK ARCHITECT

Last-mile failed delivery attempts represent an estimated $30B+ global annual operational drain for logistics carriers, driving unnecessary courier transit miles, dispatch churn, and customer dissatisfaction. BRIDGE is an end-to-end, production-grade ML operations and dispatch optimization platform calculating real-time failure probabilities, generating exact local additive SHAP explanations, running counterfactual simulations, and re-optimizing delivery sequences with 2-Opt TSP routing.

The Story Behind The Title

I am currently in my second year of Computer Science Engineering at Rajalakshmi Institute of Technology, specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. But titles aside, I see myself primarily as someone who enjoys understanding the deeper mechanics behind how things function.

Technology is easy to consume. What actually excites me is the patience required to comprehend the math, the logic, and the structural decisions beneath software and artificial intelligence. I don't pretend to know everything — far from it. What I do possess is the willingness to sit with unfamiliar, difficult problems until they finally make sense.
